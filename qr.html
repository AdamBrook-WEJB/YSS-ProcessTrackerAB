Putting it on the sheet

  1. Download the PNG.
  2. Open the tracker template in Excel, go to the PROCESS TRACK NEW tab.
  3. Insert → Pictures → This Device, pick the PNG.
  4. Drag it into the empty space to the right of the Rejection / Notes box, about 35 mm square.
  5. Copy and paste it onto each page of the template so every sheet carries one.

Print it at 25 mm or bigger. Anything smaller struggles once the sheet has been round the shop a few times.
